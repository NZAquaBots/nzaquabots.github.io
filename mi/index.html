Me Hanga Tātou!

1

Tāuta ngā Pātene Pana

KIA MATAARA → Kia anga whakarunga ngā tohu C NO NC kua tāngia ki ngā whakakā! Me āta titiro i mua i tō panuku tonu!

Panapanaia ngā pātene e rua ki ngā kōhao PCB kei te taha o ngā tohu UP me DOWN o te PCB.

2

Whakaurua te PCB i te Pouaka

Kia tūpato, āta whakaurua o te PCB ki roto i te 3D printed case, kia kuhu ai ngā pātene mā ngā kohao o mua.

3

Tirohia te whakaritenga

Tirohia kia tika te whakarārangi o ngā wāhanga katoa, ā, kia tōtika hoki ngā pātene. Ka taea te whakamahi ētahi tīwiri hei pupuri i te PCB.

4

Tūhoto ngā Pātene Pana

Āta tirohia → Me anga whakarunga tonu ngā tohu C NO NC!

Tūhoto ngā pātene kia mau. Tangohia te PCB i te pouaka i muri i tēnei mahi.

5

Whakatūria ngā Whakakā

E rua ngā whakakā tūnga-toru. Whakatūria ki ngā kōhao o te PCB kei te taha o ngā tohu PORT me STBD. Kāore he take mō te ahunga o ngā whakakā.

6

Whitikiria te Taupoki

Whitikiria te taupoki ki runga i ngā whakakā e rua kia pupuri ai i te tūnga tika. Hurihia koaro te PCB me te taupoki, ā, whakatakotoria ki runga i te pouaka ki te noho whakararo taupoki.

7

Tūhoto ngā Whakakā

Puritia te PCB kia papatahi (tērā pea ma tētahi atu e āwhina), tūhototia ngā whakakā. I ētahi wā he pai ake te tūhoto i tētahi waewae kotahi o te whakakā, kātahi ka hiki ake te papa hei tirotiro mēnā kei te tōtika.

8

Tāutatia te hononga RJ45 me te Tūnga Katiara

Pēhia te hononga RJ45 me te Tūnga Katiara, ko te manako kia tuturu te mau.

9

Tūhoto te RJ45 me te Tūnga Katiara

Hurihia te PCB, kātahi ka tūhoto i te hononga RJ45 me te Tūnga Katiara.

10

Whakaritea ngā Waea Pūhiko

Huraina te hiako (tata ki te 5mm) i ngā pito o te waea mō te hononga pūhiko, ā, takawiria ngā pito o te waea kia tōtika ai ngā whenu katoa. Kātahi, kia āta whakauru ngā waea ki ngā kōhao o te PCB.

11

Whakauru ngā Waea Pūhiko

Kia āta hurihia kōaro te PCB, me te pupuri tonu i ngā waea kia noho pūmau ki tō rātou wāhi. Tērā pea kia takitahi te whakauru i ngā waea mēnā ka paheke tonu ki waho.

12

Tūhoto me te Poro i ngā Waea

Tūhototia ngā waea kia pūmau ai ki ō rātou wāhi, ā, poroa ngā pito mēnā e roa rawa ana te puta ki waho.

13

Herea ngā Waea ki te Here Taura

Retia ngā waea i tētahi here taura , tata ki te 25mm te tawhiti mai i te wāhi i tūhototia ai ki te PCB. Mā konei e whakamutu i te kumetanga o ngā waea i te hononga tūhoto, kia kore ai te hononga e whati i a koe e toa ana i te whakataetae!

14

Tāuta te katiara

Pēhia te katiara kia noho ki roto i te tūnga katiara.

15

Whakamau te PCB ki roto i te pouaka

Whakatūria anō te PCB ki roto i te 3D printed case, ā, whakamaua ki ngā tīwiri e whā.

16

Katia te pouaka

Me whakarite kia takoto ngā waea ki runga i te kōhao o runga o te pouaka, kātahi ka whitiki te taupoki, ā, whakamaua ki ngā tīwiri e whā.

Spade Connector Steps

17

Tin Spade Connector

Using a clip to hold a spade terminal connector, tin the wire connection by heating it up with the soldering iron and adding solder.

18

Prepare and Tin Wire

Strip about 5mm of insulation from the ends of the wire for the battery connection and tin the end of the wire with a little solder.

19

Solder Wire to Spade Connector

Slide the insulated sleeve onto the wire first, then solder the wire to the spade terminal connector by heating up the solder again and holding the wire in place until all of the solder is melted. Remove the heat and hold the wire in place for at least 5 seconds, or until the solder has solidified.

20

Crimp Wire Tabs

Using a pair of needle-nose pliers, bend the wire crimp tabs on the spade terminal down so they squish the wire insulation a little bit.

21

Install Insulated Sleeve

Slide the insulated sleeve over the spade terminal connector and crimp tabs, then you can repeat steps 17-21 for the other wire.

Croc Clip Steps

22

Tin Croc Clip

Using a clip to hold a croc clip, tin the wire connection by heating it up with the soldering iron and adding solder.

23

Prepare Wire for Croc Clip

Strip about 5mm of insulation from the ends of the wire for the battery connection and tin the end of the wire with a little solder.

24

Crimp Wire to Croc Clip

Slide the insulated croc clip sleeve onto the wire first, then holding the wire in place squeeze the wire crimp tabs down so they squish the wire insulation a little bit.

25

Solder Croc Clip Connection

Solder the wire to the croc clip by heating up the solder again and holding the wire in place until all of the solder is melted.

26

Finish Croc Clip Assembly

Slide the insulated croc clip sleeve over the wire crimp tabs, then you can repeat steps 22-26 for the other wire.

Whakamātauhia tō roumamao

Ināianei, whakamātauhia tō roumamao ki a tika te haere!

1

Whakaritea te mita-maha

Mā te whakamahi i tētahi mita-maha matihiko, whakaritea te inenga ki te tohu Ω. Pā atu ngā pito ine o te mita-maha ki a rāua anō, ā, tirohia kia heke te pānui ki te 0 (tata rānei ki te 0).

2

Whakamātauhia ngā pito o te roumamao

Tūhonoa ngā pito e rua o te mita-maha ki ngā pito o te roumamao. Me tohu te mita i te nama mutunga kore (i ētahi wā ka whakaatūria hei “OC” i runga i te mita-maha).

3

Whakamātauhia ngā Pātene me ngā Whakakā

Me pēnei te pehitanga o ngā pātene e rua; Pēhia te pātene tuatahi, whai muri iho, pēhia te mea tuaruaa. Kei te pēnei hoki ngā whakakā. i. Me noho ōrite tonu te mita-maha ka tika.

4

Whakamātauhia ki te Karetao Hiko Rukuwai

Tūhonoa te roumamao ki tō Karetao Hiko Rukuwai, ā, whakaotihia anōtia taua tirohanga . I tēnei wā, me noho mutunga kore te mita maha i te wā kāore he pātene e pēhi ana, ā, mēnā ka pēhi he pātene, me heke te mita maha - tata ki te 5 ohm te pānui.

Tirohia ō tūhoto

Tuatahi – tirohia ki tō mahi tūhoto, kia kore ai tētahi hononga e pā atu ki tētahi atu. Mēnā ka kitea he hononga hē, whakamahana anō i te tūhoto mā te pūwera tūhoto mā, ā, tukuna kia heke atu te tūhoto tāpiri kia kore ai e hono ki tētahi atu hononga.

Tirohia te ahunga o ngā whakakā

Tirohia kia anga whakarunga ngā tohu C NO NC kua tāngia ki ngā pātene pana. Mēnā kāore, whakamahana marire i te tūhoto o ngā waewae e toru o te whakakā, kātahi ka huri kia tika te ahunga. Tērā pea me āwhina tētahi atu hoa i a koe, me whai i tētahi atu pūwera tūhoto hoki.

Tirohia tō Karetao Hiko Rukuwai
